\section{Reproducibility}
\label{sec:appendix-repro}

\footnotesize
\sloppy
\setlength{\emergencystretch}{3em}

We provide the following artifacts for reproducibility.

\paragraph{Artifacts.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{2pt}
\item \textbf{Code repository} (MIT licence) at \url{github.com/m-ibrahim-khalil/flood-segmentation}; the \texttt{v1.0-ijdrr} tag is the camera-ready commit. Zenodo DOI: \texttt{[to be minted at release]}.
\item \textbf{Pre-trained checkpoints} (\texttt{*.pt} PyTorch state-dicts) under \texttt{code/runs/3fold/}: 14 configurations $\times$ 3 folds $= 42$ checkpoints, $\sim 700$\,MB total.
\item \textbf{ONNX exports} (FP32 and INT8, per fold) for teacher and three students under \texttt{code/exports/}; $\sim 150$\,MB total.
\item \textbf{Per-fold metrics} in \texttt{results.json} (FP32) and \texttt{quantized.json} (INT8) under \texttt{code/runs/3fold/}; re-derivable by \texttt{run\_full\_experiment.py}.
\item \textbf{Per-platform latency} in \texttt{lat\_m2.json} and \texttt{lat\_wasm.json}; re-derivable by \texttt{bench\_m2.py} and \texttt{bench.mjs} respectively.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Environment.}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{2pt}
\item Random seed: 42, fixed in \texttt{floodlite/utils.py} and in the \texttt{KFold} constructor.
\item Software: Python 3.11; PyTorch 2.8 with CUDA 12.1 (Kaggle T4) or Apple-Silicon MPS (M2); \texttt{segmentation-models-pytorch} $\geq 0.4$; \texttt{timm} 1.0; \texttt{onnxruntime} 1.22.1 (M2 CPU; INT8 QDQ artifacts require $\geq 1.18$); \texttt{onnxruntime-web} 1.17.3 (browser).
\item Hardware: training on a single Kaggle Tesla T4 (16\,GB); quantization and CPU latency on an Apple M2~Pro (10-core CPU, 16\,GB unified memory, macOS 25.2); WASM latency via Node.js~25.
\item Estimated reproduction cost: $\sim 35$ Kaggle-T4 hours $+ \sim 2$ M2-CPU hours; wall-clock $\approx 3$ calendar days assuming no Kaggle queue.
\end{itemize}
